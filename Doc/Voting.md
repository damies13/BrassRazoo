# Voting

## Who is eligible to vote?
Each stake has one vote.

Automated voting is perfromed by staking serevrs on behalf of the stake holders.

Manual voting requires action by the stake holders, a stake holder may assign all stakes to one vote outcome or apply as many stakes as they wish to each outcome

## What is the quorum required for a vote to pass?

In order to pass a vote requires a two thirds majority (greater than 66%) to agree on the outcome

## How are "vetoes" handled?

there re no vetoes

## Automated voting

The following can be resolved with automated voting:
- decision on if a record should be reversed because it doesn't follow the rules

## Manual voting

The following requires Manual voting:
- agreeing on the introduction of new rules
- agreeing on the introduction of new tasks
